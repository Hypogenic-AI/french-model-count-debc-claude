# Downloaded Papers

## Core Papers on Number Representation in LLMs

1. **Mechanistic Interpretability of Large-Scale Counting in LLMs** (2601.02989)
   - Authors: Various
   - Year: 2026
   - arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.02989
   - Why relevant: Shows counting is layerwise, saturates at ~10 items; provides CountScope probing methodology

2. **Pre-trained Language Models Learn Remarkably Accurate Representations of Numbers** (2506.08966)
   - Authors: Kadlcik, Stefanik, Mickus, Spiegel, Kuchar
   - Year: 2025 (EMNLP)
   - arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08966
   - Why relevant: Novel sinusoidal probe achieves near-perfect number decoding from embeddings

3. **Understanding Addition and Subtraction in Transformers** (2402.02619)
   - Authors: Various
   - Year: 2024
   - arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02619
   - Why relevant: Mechanistic analysis of arithmetic circuits in transformers

4. **Unravelling the Mechanisms of Manipulating Numbers in Language Models** (2510.26285)
   - Authors: Stefanik et al.
   - Year: 2025
   - arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26285
   - Why relevant: Universal sinusoidal representations across models; cross-context probing methodology

5. **Language Models Do Not Embed Numbers Continuously** (2510.08009)
   - Authors: Davies et al.
   - Year: 2025
   - arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08009
   - Why relevant: Shows per-digit circular representations in base 10; explains digit-based errors

6. **Language Models Encode Numbers Using Digit Representations in Base 10** (2410.11781)
   - Authors: Levy, Geva
   - Year: 2024
   - arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.11781
   - Why relevant: Foundational work on base-10 digit-level circular probes; causal interventions on digit representations

## Papers on Cross-Linguistic and Compositional Number Processing

7. **Investigating the Interaction of Linguistic and Mathematical Reasoning Using Multilingual Number Puzzles** (2506.13886)
   - Authors: Bhattacharya, Papadimitriou, Davidson, Alvarez-Melis
   - Year: 2025 (EMNLP)
   - arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.13886
   - Why relevant: Most directly relevant — shows LLMs fail at implicit compositional operators in numeral systems (exactly what French vigesimal requires)

8. **Tokenization Counts: The Impact of Tokenization on Arithmetic in Frontier LLMs** (2402.14903)
   - Authors: Singh, Strouse
   - Year: 2024
   - arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14903
   - Why relevant: Tokenization direction affects arithmetic; French words tokenize differently from digits

## Papers on Number Understanding Benchmarks

9. **Number Cookbook: Number Understanding of Language Models and How to Improve It** (2411.03766)
   - Authors: GraphPKU
   - Year: 2024 (ICLR 2025)
   - arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.03766
   - Why relevant: NUPA benchmark with digit-match metric; framework adaptable for French evaluation

10. **A Fragile Number Sense** (2509.06332)
    - Authors: Rahman, Mishra
    - Year: 2025
    - arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.06332
    - Why relevant: Shows LLM number sense is fragile pattern-matching; French irregularities may expose additional fragilities

11. **What is a Number, That a Large Language Model May Know It?** (2502.01540)
    - Authors: Marjieh, Veselovsky, Griffiths, Sucholutsky
    - Year: 2025
    - arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.01540
    - Why relevant: String-number entanglement in representations; French vigesimal words create distinctive string patterns

12. **Mechanistic Evaluation of Transformers and State Space Models** (2505.15105)
    - Authors: Arora et al. (Stanford)
    - Year: 2025
    - arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15105
    - Why relevant: Causal intervention methodology applicable to studying French number processing
